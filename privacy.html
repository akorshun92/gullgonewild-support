Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Gull Gone Wild ("the game") is developed by Oleksandr Korshun. This policy explains what data the game accesses and how it is used.

Summary

Gull Gone Wild has no user accounts, and we (the developer) do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. The game shows non-personalized ads served by Google AdMob, which receives limited device and usage data to deliver those ads (see Advertising). The "No ads" purchase turns off ads between runs. We do not use analytics SDKs.

What the game does not ask for

The game does not ask for your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob between runs. It also offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one to revive, double the coins from a run, or get a handful of coins. Rewarded ads only play when you tap to watch them.

  • The game requests non-personalized ads only. Ads are not based on your interests or your activity in other apps.
  • The game does not ask for permission to track you, does not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not share data with data brokers.
  • To deliver ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud, Google may receive information such as your IP address (used for approximate location), device and operating system type, app version, and interactions with ads. This data is handled by Google under its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • We do not send Google your progress, scores, or purchase details.

Where local law requires it, the game asks for your choice before it requests any ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, a consent form provided by Google appears when you first open the game. In US states with applicable privacy laws, that form instead lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. If either applies to you, a Privacy options button appears in the game's settings, where you can review or change your answer at any time.

In-App Purchases

Purchases (looks, "No ads" and coin packs) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best score and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your Game Center account details.

Data Stored On Your Device

Your progress (best scores, coins, looks, daily tasks), your settings (language, sound, vibration) and your purchase status are stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to us or to any third party. Deleting the game deletes it.

Children's Privacy

Gull Gone Wild is intended for players aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The ads shown in the game are non-personalized, so they are not targeted based on a player's behavior. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us using the address below.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
