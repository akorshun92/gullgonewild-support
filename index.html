Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Shop and tap "Restore purchases". Looks and "No ads" bought on this Apple ID before are unlocked again at no extra cost. Coin packs are used up when bought and can't be restored.

What does "No ads" remove?

It turns off the ads that show between runs. Rewarded ads stay available as an option: you choose to watch one to revive, double your coins or get a handful of coins.

I lost my progress after reinstalling the game.

Your progress (coins, looks, records) is stored only on your device, so deleting the game deletes it too. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID: use "Restore purchases" in the Shop to get them back. Your best score stays on the Game Center leaderboard.

Why don't I see the leaderboard or achievements?

They use Game Center. Make sure you're signed in under Settings → Game Center on your iPhone, then reopen the game.

Where can I change my ad privacy choice?

If you're in a region where the game asks for consent before showing ads (for example the EU or the UK), a "Privacy options" button appears in the game's settings. You can review or change your answer there at any time.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. You can play without an internet connection. Ads, purchases and Game Center simply wait until you're back online.
